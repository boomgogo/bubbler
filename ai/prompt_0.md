





# Features

1. **Teach the specials.** A one-line hint the first time a Comet, Nova, Mist or Obsidian bubble appears. Today they are only explained in the pause menu.
2. **More places.** A new environment every 3 levels, each loaded as its own chunk after play starts. The cube-map reflection picks it up with no shader change.
    - Change the background sound to a music that matches the environment
3. When starting each level, do a fly over the column of bubbles, zoom in on a few bubbles to show the rendering details
