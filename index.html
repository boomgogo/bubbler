How to play
  • Aim and release. Three of a colour pop; anything left hanging falls for double.
  • Tap the launcher to swap the two bubbles in hand.
  • A ringed Comet clears its row when popped. A white Nova bursts when touched.
  • Mist hides a colour until something pops beside it. Black Obsidian only falls or shatters.
  • Gems arrive when you are stuck. Spend them on the two boosts beside the launcher.
